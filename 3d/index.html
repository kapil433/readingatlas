A NOTE BEFORE YOU WANDER

Make yourself at home.

01

Choose a place.

Enter a floor, choose a named country or subject in the directory, or search all entries. Each book has a permanent shelf address.

02

Walk and look.

Drag the scene to look around. Use W A S D or arrow keys to walk; Q and E turn. Touch arrows work too.

03

Follow your curiosity.

Select a book to inspect it, read the existing notes or save it to your desk. “Show route” marks the way; “Visit shelf” takes you there.

04

Explore your way.

The complete catalogue and directory work with a keyboard. Reduced-motion preferences are respected, and low detail helps on smaller devices.

About this collection

1,412 catalogue entries across nine collections, including 50 author reading paths. Covers show matched editions where available; a typographic cover identifies entries without confirmed artwork.
